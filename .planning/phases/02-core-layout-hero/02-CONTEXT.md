**Gathered:** 2026-01-20
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Deliver the site shell (header, navigation, language toggle) and the hero section — the primary conversion point where visitors decide to engage or bounce. Navigation to other pages, footer, and content sections are separate phases.

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Hero messaging
- Tone: Bold and direct — punchy, confident statements (e.g., "We build your startup's tech. You keep the equity.")
- Target audience: Both technical and non-technical founders — neutral language that works for all
- No hand-holding tone or "first-time founder" emphasis; assume competent adults

### Claude's Discretion
The user explicitly granted Claude full discretion on the following to optimize for 2025-2026 conversion best practices and the premium minimal aesthetic:

**Hero messaging:**
- Equity model framing — whether to lead with it or position as supporting proof point
- Service specificity — high-level vs. light keyword hints (AI, web, mobile)

**CTA design:**
- Primary CTA copy — innovative/surprising approach welcome; must fit bold direct tone
- Secondary action — scroll prompt, email capture, or single focused CTA
- Duration display — whether to show "30-min" in button text
- Button styling — solid, outline, gradient, or animated; match black/white brand

**Visual hierarchy:**
- Hero layout — centered stack, split layout, or full-bleed text
- Imagery approach — abstract/geometric, product mockup, text-only, or hybrid
- Above-fold balance and information density

**Header behavior:**
- Fixed vs scroll behavior
- Mobile menu style (hamburger, slide-out, etc.)
- Logo placement and sizing
- Language toggle design and placement

</decisions>
